# Privacy Policy for Setlist

Last updated: 2026-11-09

Setlist ("the app") is a read-only app for viewing songs and setlists, including a live mode for performing a setlist. It is published by jadevelopr ("I", "me").

## What the app collects

The app does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or enter any personal information. It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or advertising ID. It contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking libraries.

## What the app does with data

When you open the app it downloads the list of songs and setlists (titles, artists, keys, tempos and song structures) from a server hosted by Supabase (https://supabase.com). This content is band repertoire data, not data about you. Nothing is stored on your device beyond what the app needs while it is running, and nothing you do in the app is sent back to me.

## Information sent automatically

Like any app that connects to the internet, each request reveals your device's IP address and standard request details (such as the app's user agent) to the server. Supabase may keep these in its server logs for security and operation purposes. I do not use this information to identify you. See Supabase's privacy policy at https://supabase.com/privacy.

## Sharing

I do not sell or share personal information with third parties. The only service involved is Supabase, which hosts the song data as described above.

## Security

All connections use HTTPS. The app does not allow unencrypted traffic.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

## Your rights

Because the app does not collect personal information from you, there is no personal data held by me to access, correct or delete. If you have questions or requests about privacy, contact me at the address below.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same address.
